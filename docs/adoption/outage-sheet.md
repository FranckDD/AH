title: When the system does not respond
subtitle: Stick this sheet near every workstation
---

## First checks (2 minutes)

1. Look at the indicator at the top right. **Connected** is green. **Offline** is yellow.
2. Check the network: cable, Wi-Fi, router lights. Is another computer also affected?
3. Reload the page once. Wait 1 minute. Try again.
4. Still nothing? Write the time, then follow the next section for your role.

## What still works without network

- **Reception, nurses, doctors:** patients, invoices, withdrawals, consultations, prescriptions and appointments are kept on the computer and sent later. The indicator reads "Offline (your entries are saved)".
- **Laboratory:** you can type and validate results. Printing the PDF sheet, corrections, history and stock need the network.
- **Needs the network:** cash closing, discount requests, dashboards and totals, hospitalisation, shift calendar, stock, production, toxicology, spiritual notes, catalogue editing, reports. Offline pregnancy fields: to confirm.
- A screen that says "Cannot load" or shows zero is not empty. It cannot be read.

## Use paper when

The screen needs the network. Use the paper forms: [LOCATION OF THE FORMS]. Write the date, time, patient code or name, your name and the details. Keep the forms in the tray [LOCATION] and enter them as soon as the network is back. Mark each form "entered" with the date.

## Do not

- Do not create a second record for the same patient. Search first.
- Do not enter again a sale or entry already shown as waiting or pending.
- Do not clear the browser data, uninstall the browser or switch off the computer while entries are waiting.
- Do not admit, update or discharge a hospitalised patient while the indicator is yellow.

## Report it

Tell [NAME] [PHONE]. Give: the time, your name, the screen, the exact message, what you were doing. A photo of the screen helps.

## When the network comes back

1. The indicator turns green within a few seconds. Leave the computer on until it does.
2. Open **Sync failures** if your menu has it. Correct or delete each line shown.
3. Check that what you entered is in the file or the list, once.
4. Then enter the paper forms, and only those.
5. Cash closing: do it only after the waiting entries are sent.
