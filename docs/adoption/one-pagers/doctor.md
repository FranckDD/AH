## Every day

1. Sign in. Open the Dashboard and look at **Patients awaiting review**.
2. Select **Take in charge** on the next patient. The nurse's consultation opens with the measurements filled in.
3. Write the consultation: reason, examination, diagnosis, treatment. Save.
4. Prescribe: **New Prescription**, with medicine, dose, frequency and duration.
5. Need an exam? Order it as a laboratory order. The laboratory finds it at reception.
6. Read the results in the patient file or in **Exam Results**. A corrected result sends you a notice.
7. Book a follow-up appointment from the file.
8. Hospitalised patients: admit, record the progress, discharge and print the discharge letter.

## Good to know

- Offline, the patient file, consultations, prescriptions and appointments still work and are sent later. Taking a patient in charge, the dashboard, reading exam results and every hospitalisation screen need the network.
- Never admit, update or discharge while the indicator is yellow. The tab may wrongly say that the patient is not hospitalised.
- Exact age is shown in days, weeks or months for young children. Pregnancy status is visible to you and to the laboratory, never to reception.
- If another computer saved a newer consultation, your older one is refused and listed under **Sync failures**.

> **If something goes wrong.** "Cannot load the data": wait for the green indicator and press **Retry**. Do not trust an empty list. Write the consultation on the paper form from the outage sheet and enter it later.
