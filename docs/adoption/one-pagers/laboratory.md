## Every day

1. Sign in. Check that the indicator reads **Connected**.
2. **Reception**: find the patient. Take the doctor's order or the exams invoiced at the cash desk. Choose the name written on the order as prescriber. External patient: type the outside prescriber's name as asked on the screen.
3. Take the sample and keep the laboratory code on the tube.
4. At the bench, pick the exam and type each value. Leave empty what you did not measure.
5. Check the flags. Write the conclusion if the doctor must read one.
6. **Save Draft** if the analyser is not finished. **Validate & Close** when the values are final.
7. **VALIDATE & PRINT** gives the result sheet in the laboratory's own format. Printing needs the network.
8. Once a week: **Lab count** of the laboratory stock, then **Submit count**.

## Head of laboratory only

- You can edit the exam catalogue and its parameters. Check the units and reference values before you save. This needs the network.
- Look at corrected results and weekly counts with the team.

## Good to know

- Results can be typed offline and are sent later. PDF printing, correcting a validated result, the history and all stock screens need the network.
- Offline, a case may show age and sex as "-". Do not rely on the reference column until you are online.
- Age appears in days, weeks or months for young children. Pregnancy status, when recorded, is visible to you and to clinical staff, never to reception.

> **If something goes wrong.** A validated result is wrong: **Correct this result** and give the reason. The doctor is told. Entry refused after the network returns (for example an exam already received): read **Sync failures**. Keep the paper bench sheet and enter the values later. Stock count fails: keep the numbers on screen and retry without reloading.
