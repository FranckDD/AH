## Every day and every week

1. Sign in. Choose the period at the top of **Reports**.
2. Read the **daily check**: the box lists the points that deserve a look. Use **Open the screen** on a point to see the detail.
3. Read the cards: money, products, production, growth. Look at the total sold per product.
4. Decide the **discount requests** and the staff discounts. Type your password to confirm. An approval needs a percentage or a due date.
5. Read the **cash closings** and decide on cash adjustment requests.
6. Follow the laboratory stock alerts and the production.
7. Validate toxicology discharges when asked.
8. Export the Reports page as PDF for the weekly meeting.
9. Once a week, send the short staff note and hold the 15-minute review.

## Good to know

- The nightly check sends a notification only for something new.
- You can read the spiritual counsellors' notes (read only). Open them only when needed.
- All your screens need the network.

> **If something goes wrong.** Cards at zero or "Cannot load": the network. Retry later. A day without an evening closing shows in the check: ask the secretary. Locked after five wrong passwords (10 minutes): wait, or ask the admin to unlock. A PDF does not download: a download manager on the computer may be blocking it.
