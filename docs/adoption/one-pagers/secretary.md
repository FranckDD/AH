## Every day

1. Sign in. Wait until the top right indicator reads **Connected**.
2. Search the patient by name or code. Not found? Register the patient once, with the national identity number if there is one.
3. Book or check appointments in **Appointments**.
4. Create the invoice: choose the patient, then **scan the barcode** on each product, or search it by name. Prices come from the catalogue and are locked.
5. Choose the category and the payment method, type the amount received, then **Save Transaction**. Give the receipt.
6. Part payment? Add an instalment later on the same invoice. Never make a second invoice for the same sale.
7. Record each cash payout in **Withdrawals** with a clear reason.
8. At the end of the day open **Cash closing**, count, type each total and **Validate the closing**.

## Good to know

- You never see pregnancy fields or medical results. This is on purpose.
- Offline, patients, invoices and withdrawals are kept on the computer and sent later. A new patient shows "CODE EN ATTENTE" until the code arrives.
- Cash closing and discount requests need the network. A closing is refused while entries are still waiting to be sent.
- Keep the computer on and do not clear the browser until the indicator is green again.

> **If something goes wrong.** Nothing responds: read the outage sheet near your desk. Unknown barcode: search the product by name. Entry refused after the network returns: open **Sync failures**, then correct or delete it. Five wrong passwords: the account is locked for 10 minutes. Wait, or ask the admin to unlock it.
