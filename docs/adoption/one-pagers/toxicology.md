## Every day

1. Sign in. Open **Toxicology Monitoring**. If you read "Cannot load the data while offline", the network is down: use paper.
2. Manager: **New Toxico Admission**. Search the patient first, so that you do not create a second one. Choose the substance and the referral psychologist, give the guardian's name and phone, attach the signed consent, **Validate Admission**.
3. Psychologist: find one of your patients, **Evaluate**, choose Maintain, Progression or Regression, write the observations, **Submit Evaluation**.
4. Manager: discharge when the criteria are met, readmit if needed, transfer a patient to another psychologist with a reason.
5. Assistant: read the files and admit patients as your account allows.
6. Print the file or the list when required.

## Good to know

- The toxicology screens keep nothing on the computer. Keep interview notes on paper and enter them when the badge is green.
- Evaluation text is clinical and confidential. Close your session when you leave the desk.
- The promoter sees the decision and the dates, not your observations.
- Some psychologists may see only their own patients, as set by the administrator.

> **If something goes wrong.** Empty list or dashes: that is the network, not the real situation. Press **Retry**. Consent scan refused: reduce it under 5 MB. Patient already in the list: do not admit twice, open the existing file. Two people with the same name: check the code and the birth date.
