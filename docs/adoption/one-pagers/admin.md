## Every day

1. Sign in. Check the notifications bell.
2. **Users**: create an account for each new person, with the right role. Give the first password in person.
3. Forgotten password: open the account, type a new one, give it in person. There is no reset by e-mail.
4. Switch off the account of a person who leaves. Do not delete it.
5. After five wrong passwords an account is locked for 10 minutes. Unlock it if the person cannot wait [to confirm: the screen for unlocking].
6. Decide discount requests and **stock adjustment requests** from the notifications.
7. Check **Audit and Security**: who did what, and failed sign-ins with their reason.
8. Keep prices, the exam catalogue, product photos and label sizes up to date. Prices are locked for the secretary.
9. Before the training, create training accounts. Delete them before go-live.

## Good to know

- Your administration screens need the network.
- The audit log records who did what, for traceability and safety, not for punishment. Read it only for a reason.
- The spiritual notes screen is read only and confidential.

> **If something goes wrong.** A save fails: check the network first. A person gets "wrong password" on a switched off account: the screen shows the same message, so read the sign-in log. A deleted account cannot be recovered: create it again. After an outage, go through the sync failures with the team.
