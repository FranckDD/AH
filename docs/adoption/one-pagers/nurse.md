## Every day

1. Sign in. Check that the indicator reads **Connected**.
2. New patient: **Patients**, **Add a patient**, fill the name, birth date and gender, **Create**. Write the code in the patient's paper notebook.
3. Open the patient file. Take the measurements and write the nursing consultation, then **Save**.
4. Check in the file that the consultation really appears before the patient leaves.
5. Patient to see a doctor: send the patient to the doctor's waiting list from the consultation.
6. Record prescriptions and appointments from the file or from **Appointments**.
7. Read laboratory results in the patient file when they are ready.
8. Patient who stays in the centre: admit, record the progress each shift, discharge when the doctor decides.
9. Head nurse: plan the rotation in the shift calendar.

## Good to know

- Offline, the patient list, the files and new consultations still work and are sent later. The dashboard, the waiting list, the shift calendar and the hospitalisation screens need the network. Do not admit or discharge while the indicator is yellow.
- You see the clinical side only. Pregnancy status, when recorded, is visible to you and to the laboratory, never to reception.
- Only the latest consultation can be corrected.

> **If something goes wrong.** Screen empty or "Cannot load the data": that is the network, not an empty list. Press **Retry** when the indicator is green. A patient or consultation refused after the network returns is listed under **Sync failures**: enter it again from the text shown. Use the paper form from the outage sheet and enter it later.
