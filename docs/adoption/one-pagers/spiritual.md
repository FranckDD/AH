## Every day

1. Sign in. Open the dashboard.
2. Read the list of followed patients if you need to know who is in which phase.
3. After a meeting, select **New note**.
4. Check the meeting date. It shows today.
5. Tick what was done: counsel, prayer, verse read, discussion, text given, questions and answers. Tick **People did not want to talk** if that was the case. It is a normal outcome.
6. Add free text if you wish, up to 5000 characters. The text is optional.
7. Select **Save**. Wait for "Note saved."
8. Later you can edit or delete your own notes.

## Good to know

- The notes list has no link to a patient. Write a name only if you really need it.
- Notes are read by the spiritual counsellors, the promoter and the administrator (read only). Nobody else.
- Saving a note needs the network. The yellow badge stays yellow for your role and says nothing about the network. Judge by what the screen shows.

> **If something goes wrong.** Empty list or "0 patients followed": that is the network. Reload and wait. A note will not save: keep the text, wait for the connection and press **Save** again, or keep it on paper and enter it later.
