## Every day

1. Sign in. Check that the indicator reads **Connected**. These screens have no offline mode.
2. **Inputs and purchases**: record each purchase with quantity and price. Fix or cancel a mistaken one.
3. Watch the alert threshold of each input. The bell tells you when one is low.
4. **New batch**: choose the finished product, check the date, choose the inputs, **Create batch**.
5. Start production, then enter the results when the batch is done.
6. Bottles that leave before shipping (sample or other)? **Declare the output** with the quantity and a note.
7. **Validate balance and ship**: choose the delivery date and the expiry date. If the shortfall is out of tolerance, write the reason.
8. Check the tracking page for anomalies.

## Good to know

- Shipping creates the production lot that stock then receives and checks.
- Declared outputs need no approval but must be true. Good bottles are removed before shipping.
- A step that does not pass leaves the batch in its previous status. Nothing is half done.

> **If something goes wrong.** "Could not save. Try again.": keep the form open, wait for the green indicator and press the button again, then check the list so a purchase is not recorded twice. Empty list: it does not mean there is no stock, reload first. Write the batch figures on paper meanwhile.
