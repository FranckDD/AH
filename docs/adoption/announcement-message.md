## Questions and answers

**Will this replace me?**
No. The system does the writing and adding up. Care, laboratory work, counting and decisions stay with you. No job is removed because of this project [promoter to confirm this commitment before sending].

**Is my work being watched?**
The system keeps an audit log that records who did what and when. It exists for safety and traceability, for example to find where an error started. It is not used to punish. Only the people in charge of the system read it, and only for a reason [to confirm who].

**What if I make a mistake?**
Tell your champion. Most entries can be corrected or cancelled, and the correction is recorded. A validated laboratory result is corrected with a reason. A closed cash closing is changed by an adjustment request.

**What if there is no network?**
Reception, nursing, medical and laboratory result entry keep working and send the data later. For the rest, use paper and enter it when the network returns. See the outage sheet near your desk.

**Do I still keep paper?**
Yes, during the pilot. Paper and system run in parallel until the date set by the promoter. After that, paper is kept only for the outage forms.

**Who do I ask for help?**
First your champion [NAME]. Then the support person [NAME] [PHONE].

**Can I give ideas?**
Yes. Tell your champion, write in the issues log, or speak at the weekly 15-minute review.

**When is the paper stopped?**
Service by service, when the go/no-go criteria in the plan are met for that service. The date for each service is [DATE]. You will be told at least [X] days before.
